## :wrench: Conclusions

These are conclusions for now, given our resources and state.
They can be subject to change in the future.

For Unikraft core components (`unikraft`, `app-elfloader` and `lib-` repositories) we follow a company-centric model.
Contributions coming from the company will be prioritized to be reviewed and merged.

We keep the door open for external contributions, but we make it known, both implicitly and explicitly that they are going to be handled best effort (for reviewing and merging).

Maintainers for core components will be part of the company.
It is difficult to have external maintainers and give them the required attention.

Contributions are welcome to non-core components (catalogs, docs, governance, testing, CI), particularly catalogs.
External maintainers can be assigned to non-core components.

The core development process is inherently slower because of its critical part in the ecosystem.
Faster development is part of non-core components (KraftKit, catalogs).
These faster contributions can be used as input for buzz-making.
